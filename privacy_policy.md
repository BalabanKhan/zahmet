# Privacy Policy for Zahmet.

**Effective Date:** 2026-06-06

This Privacy Policy explains how Zahmet ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you use our mobile application ("Zahmet").

### 1. Information Collection and Use

**Zahmet does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.** 
All information you provide to the app, including but not limited to:
- The tasks you create.
- The times you complete or postpone tasks.
- The photos you take using the camera to verify task completion.

...remains strictly and securely stored locally on your own device. We do not have access to any of this data.

### 2. Camera Access

Zahmet requests access to your device's camera (`NSCameraUsageDescription`). This permission is solely used to allow you to take photos to visually verify the completion of your tasks. 
- Photos taken within the app are processed locally on your device.
- We do not upload, share, or analyze your photos externally.

### 3. Analytics and Tracking

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks that collect your usage data or personal information.

### 4. Data Security

Because your data never leaves your device, its security is guaranteed by your device’s operating system. If you delete the Zahmet application, all of your tasks and related data will be permanently deleted from your device.

### 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Since we do not collect your email address or personal contact information, we will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.

### 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact the developer.
